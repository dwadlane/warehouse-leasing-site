07 / Frequently Asked

Common Questions

Straight answers before you reach out. Still have one? Use the form below or message us on WhatsApp.

What is the minimum lease term?

Terms are flexible from 1 to 9 years, with an exclusive option to renew. Shorter pilot terms are considered for qualified tenants case by case.

Can the space be demised for multiple tenants?

Yes. The facility can be split for 2 to 4 operators, minimum take-up around 2,000 SQM each, with independent access and a separate lease per tenant. All splits are illustrative and subject to landlord approval and final survey.

Can I purchase the pre-installed racking separately?

Yes, for tenants who choose the Bare Shell rate. The racking system can be purchased outright rather than paying the Racking-Ready monthly premium. Ask us for current pricing.

What is the truck ban situation on C5?

Smaller delivery vehicles can operate 24/7 from this location, bypassing C5 heavy vehicle truck ban hours entirely. This is a genuine last mile advantage for e-commerce and urban distribution fleets.

What power and water supply does the facility have?

3-phase industrial power, a 254mm industrial water mainline plus an on-site reservoir, and a GPS-synchronized seismic early warning system.

What condition is the facility handed over in?

Immediate occupancy, racking pre-installed and operational, NFPA 13 fire suppression live, dual-layer security active. Build-to-spec fit-out, including loading dock work and office build-out, is available on request.
